Why Sydney?

Sydney is a vibrant, clean, and beautiful city with the perfect mix of busy city life and relaxing beaches. It is also very diverse, with many different cuisines, neighborhoods, and activities to explore.

Before You Go

Preparing ahead of time can make moving to Sydney much easier. Make sure you have your travel documents, money, phone plan, and packing figured out before you leave.

What to Pack

Remember that Australia's seasons are opposite from the United States, so check the weather for the time of year you will be there.

  • Comfortable clothes and walking shoes
  • Swimsuit and beach clothes
  • Light jacket or warmer layers
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
  • Australian power adapter
  • Passport and travel documents

Other Things to Prepare

Check your visa requirements, tell your bank you will be traveling, and decide whether you will use an international phone plan or Australian SIM card.

University Life

University in Australia may be different from what you are used to in the U.S. Grading scales, attendance policies, and course structures can vary, so check your university's policies before classes begin.

Classes and Grading

At UNSW, for example, 85–100 is a High Distinction, 75–84 is a Distinction, 65–74 is a Credit, and 50–64 is a Pass. Courses may also have fewer assignments that are worth a larger part of your final grade.

Choosing Courses

If possible, consider balancing harder classes with easier electives. This gives you more time to adjust, meet people, explore Sydney, and travel during your semester abroad.

Explore Sydney

Sydney has many different areas to explore. The CBD has famous landmarks like the Opera House and Harbour Bridge, while neighborhoods like Newtown and Surry Hills are great for food, cafés, shopping, and nightlife.

Beaches

Some popular beaches include Bondi, Coogee, Bronte, and Manly. The Bondi to Coogee coastal walk is also a great way to see several beaches in one day.
